# KFUPM Circle
**SWE363 · Section 01 · Team 7**

KFUPM Circle helps KFUPM students discover classmates who share their courses and interests, connect with other students, join clubs, and find companions for activities.

## Phase 4: Front-End Development

This phase focuses on implementing an interactive React front end based on our Figma prototype. The interface will support both desktop and mobile screens.

## Figma Prototype

View our Figma prototype: **(https://swe363-sec1-team7.figma.site)**

## Main Features

- Account registration and sign-in
- Student profiles and discovery
- Connections and messaging
- Clubs, activities, and invitations
- Club management
- Administration and report management
